    How I work?

    I try to understand a problem fully before I propose a solution.

    Listen firstI talk with the people who live with the problem before I write a requirement.
    Say no on purposeEvery roadmap I own has a list of things I chose not to build, with reasons.
    TranslateI explain one decision to engineers, lawyers, and executives in the words each group needs.
    Let evidence winI trust usage data and user feedback over my own favorite idea.
    Keep a person in the loopWith AI, I ask what happens when it is wrong and make sure someone can catch it.
    What I enjoy most
    At work:I use creativity and curiosity to build things that improve quality and experience, reduce frustration, and solve real problems. I like doing it with other people.
    Off the clock:I read comparative studies of literature and philosophy, and I enjoy writing essays and poetry. Sitting with complexity until it becomes clear is how I think best.
